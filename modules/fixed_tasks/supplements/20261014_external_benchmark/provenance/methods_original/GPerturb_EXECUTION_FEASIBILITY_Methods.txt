PROPOSED METHODS; NO GPERTURB TRAINING HAS BEEN PERFORMED.

We will evaluate the official Gaussian GPerturb implementation pinned to commit 05d5a431413ff9dc443c82b994b571ecc563426d. The model will receive the same shallow cells and non-targeting controls as the other methods, a target one-hot design with all-zero controls, log-normalized expression, and input-derived technical covariates. This dataset-specific input adapter will be disclosed; the model and native loss will remain unchanged. Four D3-only configurations will cross hidden widths 700 and 1,000 with learning rates 0.001 and 0.0005, retaining four hidden layers, tau=1, and the official prior settings. We will compare two readouts already used in the official examples: the continuous inclusion-weighted effect and the effect thresholded at inclusion probability 0.5. Configuration and readout selection will use the existing D3 development P@25 endpoint only.

A fixed shallow-cell validation split will determine an operationally stable epoch budget, with a minimum of 250 and maximum of 500 epochs; all decisions, split identities and model checkpoints will be archived. A transparent training wrapper is required because the official helper redraws its split, resets its optimizer on every call and does not expose Gaussian loss histories. It will be checked against the unchanged native loss and optimizer updates. We will retain full-validation native loss evaluation rather than treating arbitrary mini-batch validation as an identical GP objective. The selected configuration will be refitted on all shallow input cells so that the formal 20-cell task does not lose cells to validation. Two additional prespecified D3 seeds will assess optimization sensitivity; no best-seed selection will be used. D4 fits will transfer the locked model specification and epoch budget without reference-driven retuning.

Resource qualification will precede performance evaluation: a 10-minute implementation check and a 60-minute full-shape resource pilot, followed by at most 12 hours per formal fit and a cooperative 6.5 GiB memory guard on the current 8 GiB host. These caps do not establish adequate training. If the native full-size validation or the required stability criteria cannot be completed, GPerturb will be labelled resource-blocked or unconverged and excluded from claims of method superiority. No training on a reduced candidate or query-only gene set will substitute for the full primary task.
